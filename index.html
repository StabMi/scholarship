📱 Senator Outreach (Brother Sends All)

01
Brother Sends, Not Sibling
Letters and messages signed and sent by the student himself carry significantly more weight. Officials respond better to requests from the applicant directly.
02
Mention Meycauayan to Lito Lapid
Senator Lito Lapid took his senatorial oath in Barangay Pantoc, Meycauayan in 2019. Mention you are from Meycauayan — this personal connection may get a faster response.
03
Bokals Are Underrated
Board Members (Bokals) are often overlooked but have direct access to provincial funds and are highly accessible. BM Hernandez is especially strong — she was previously a Meycauayan councilor herself.
04
Personal Emails First
Emails to personal Gmail/Yahoo accounts go directly to the senator's team, bypassing general inboxes. Always email the personal address AND message on Facebook simultaneously.
05
Apply Everywhere at Once
Don't wait for one response before applying to the next. All these are separate funding sources — you can receive from multiple simultaneously.
06
Follow Up After 1 Week
Send a polite follow-up if no reply. Call offices directly — phone often works faster than email or Facebook for formal offices like CSWD or the Capitol.
